Stock Movement

Receiving stock increases the quantity of the selected product at the selected plant and writes a transaction record.

Issuing stock decreases the quantity. The operation is rejected if it would take the quantity below zero.

How this works
  1. The request is validated (product, plant, positive quantity).
  2. The inventory row for this product and plant is found or created.
  3. The available quantity is checked first.
  4. The quantity is updated inside a single database transaction.
  5. A transaction record is written with your email as the performer.

Stock OUT is rejected when the requested quantity is more than what is available. Nothing is changed in that case.
